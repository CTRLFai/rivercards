# River + Rail TCG

A trading card game for the UF MSE Primer. Study a module, pass checkpoints and quizzes, earn packs, collect and master the cards. Case business: River + Rail, laser cleaning, Jacksonville.

Play: https://ctrlfai.github.io/rivercards/

On iPhone, open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

- Set 1 (Module 1): Groundbreaking, 18 cards plus a Signature card earned by the graded exercise.
- Exercise grades and field missions arrive as redeem codes from the module chat. Make one with `python3 tools/rrcode.py E 1 4` (type, module, grade or count).
- Your collection is stored on the phone. Use More > Backup to copy it.
- Card designs and art are original. Frame styles borrow general conventions from classic trading card games.
